Contextual Search

Contextual search for bloomingdales.ae, built from the BLM Design System and the BLMHW Sprint 2 search designs: an animated placeholder and first-visit tip in the header, and a search modal that explains contextual search, highlights matches as you type and handles no results. Boards marked Prototype are clickable.

Desktop · 1440px

BLM notes

  • Header and search field follow the BLM Design System (Header / Status=DK, Search / Type=Contextual): Inter, #111 / #767676 / #CBCBCB.
  • On this board the sparkles replays the tip for review; in the flow, a click anywhere on the search (sparkles included) opens the search modal below.
  • Placeholder cycles BLM-specific queries and scrolls on one line, never wraps or truncates.

Search modal (BLMHW Sprint 2, Desktop)

  • The whole search (icon, field and sparkles) is one target: a click anywhere opens the modal over the header. Focus goes to the field.
  • Search box: #F9F9F9, 72px, “Search the way you’d describe it”, 24px search + 24px close (no sparkles in the box).
  • One grid for every state: 3 equal columns, 32px gaps (Figma Desktop), so nothing shifts while typing.
    – Empty field: Contextual search explainer + Try examples | Designers | Your recent searches (+ Clear All), or Trending if no history
    – Typing (suggestions): Designers | Results + Show All Results | Your recent searches (Trending if no history). Product results are hidden.
    – No results: Designers | “Sorry, there are no results for …” | Your recent searches.
  • Preview: toggle “hasHistory” and “noResults” in Tweaks; typing keyboard mash (e.g. asdasdasd) also triggers no results.
  • Dummy live search: Results and Designers filter from a sample catalogue as you type; the typed words or letters are highlighted in magenta (#FA16C8, per Figma; 3.4:1 on white, below AA for 16px). Clicking any result, designer, recent or trending search, or Show All Results shows a “Goes to results page” tooltip (prototype stand-in for the PLP). The Try examples still fill the field. Pressing Enter with a query shows “Goes to results page” under the search box; Enter on an empty field does nothing. Try “a black bag for evening”, “dress”, “sne” or “gucci”.
  • Overlay: 70% black; ×, Esc or clicking it closes (animated) and clears the query.

Mobile · 375px

Mobile search (BLMHW Sprint 2, Mobile)

  • Homepage header (BLM Design System): search icon + 16px sparkles as one tap target; first-visit tip (340px, “NEW • CONTEXTUAL SEARCH … Got It”) fades in after load and goes for good after Got It or opening search.
  • Tap the search: the search sheet drops over the header (same 200ms open/close as desktop), focus goes to the field, 16px text so iOS doesn’t zoom.
  • Search box: #F9F9F9, 20px padding, 20px search + close icons.
  • Sections stack in one column, same logic as desktop:
    – Empty field: Contextual search explainer + Try examples → Your recent searches (Trending if no history)
    – Typing: Results (live highlight) → Designers → Your recent searches
    – No results: “Sorry, there are no results for …” → Designers → Your recent searches
  • Results, designers, recent and trending taps show “Goes to results page”; Enter does the same under the field.
  • Long content scrolls inside the sheet; the 70% overlay fills any space below it.
  • Tweaks: startOpen, showTip, hasHistory, noResults. Device status bar and Safari bar from the Figma are left out.
Designed in Claude; this GitHub copy runs the same boards with a small stand-in runtime (source/dc-lite.js). Product, designer and result content is placeholder or demo data; links show a “Goes to results page” tooltip instead of navigating.
